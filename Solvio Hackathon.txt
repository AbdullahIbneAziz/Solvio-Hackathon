Admin Requirements
●	Admin can create, edit, and delete branches with proper login and dashboard interface.
●	Admin can view, create, edit, and delete staff accounts.
●	Admin can view detailed reports on sales, inventory, AI forecasting, customer activity, and staff.
●	Admin can filter and export reports.
●	Admin has access to a centralized dashboard showing aggregated sales, inventory, forecasting, customer activity, and top-selling products.
●	Admin can add, update, view, and delete products in the warehouse.
●	Model can suggest the admin about top product demand, sales forecasting about upcoming days, warns the admin about product shortages.
●	Admin can handle(upload, modify, delete) model file of AI forecasting (Like LSTM, ARIMA, LLM)
Staff Requirements
●	Staff can add, update, view, and delete sales within their assigned branch.
●	Staff can add new products, update stock, view inventory, and remove products from their branch inventory.
●	Staff can access a branch-level dashboard showing sales, inventory, and product insights for their branch only.
●	Staff can record, update, and view customer information relevant to their branch.
●	Staff can view and download reports specific to their branch.
●	Model can suggest the staff about top product demand, sales forecasting about upcoming days, warns the admin about product shortages.
Non-Functional Requirements
●	Secure login and user data protection using encryption.
●	Simple and clear user interface for both admin and staff.
●	Support for future expansion (more branches or users).
●	Pop-up message after actions (add, edit, delete, update).
●	Access restriction to authorized users only.
●	Ensure usability, reliability, availability, and consistent performance during multiple user operations.
